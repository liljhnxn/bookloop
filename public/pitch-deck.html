BookLoop

"Every Book Deserves Another Reader."

A zero-fee, tokenless public library protocol on Botchain Testnet (Chain ID 968) enabling communities to circulate physical literature transparently on-chain.

Smart Contract: 0x37261E07640A9B2e394d44d2FF1a2100131Fd8d0

01. The Problem

Physical Books Are Trapped in Silence

Dormant Knowledge

Over 2.5 billion physical books sit idle on shelves after a single read, slowly gathering dust.

Economic Barriers

High retail book prices restrict access for students, researchers, and avid readers globally.

Centralized Fragility

Centralized platforms exploit personal data, impose fees, or shut down when hosting costs mount.

02. The Solution

The Open On-Chain Library Protocol

  • Free & Tokenless: No platform tokens, no speculative NFTs, no paywalls.
  • 100% On-Chain Registry: Every listing, claim, and handover is permanently recorded on Botchain.
  • Physical Provenance: View the complete historical journey of each book across readers.
  • Warm Literary Experience: Built with warm beige, dark brown, and burnt orange hues designed for readers.

03. How It Works

Deterministic On-Chain State Lifecycle

1. List Book

Owner lists book title, author, and genre. Status becomes Available.

2. Claim Book

Another reader claims for free. Anti-self-claim and duplicate protection kick in. Status: Claimed.

3. Handover

Upon physical exchange, the original owner confirms collection. Status: Collected.

04. Technical Architecture

Clean, Verified, Serverless Web3 Stack

  • Solidity 0.8.20: Immutable BookLoop.sol with 14/14 automated tests passing.
  • Botchain Testnet (968): High throughput, sub-second finality, negligible gas fees.
  • Next.js 14 App Router: Single-page responsive web app with wagmi & viem.
  • Zero Backend: Zero central databases, zero single points of failure.
